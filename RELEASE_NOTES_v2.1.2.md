# Emo-Xpress 2.1.2

## Quick Picker fades in and out, and closes after each choice

- **Fade in and out:** the Quick Picker fades in when its shortcut opens it. Escape or a click outside fades it out before it closes.
- **Escape works after a mouse choice:** Escape now closes the picker even when focus is on an emoji, not the search box.
- **Click outside closes it reliably**, including after several emojis have been chosen in a row.
- **New application icon** for the executable, installer, shortcuts and taskbar.

Your data is unchanged: there is no database change in this version.
